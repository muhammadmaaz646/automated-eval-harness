# Store Policy

## Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in
original packaging. Store credit is issued for returns without a receipt.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business
days and costs an extra $9.99. We offer free shipping on orders over $50.

## Return Shipping
Return shipping is free — we email a prepaid shipping label once a return is
requested through the account portal.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days
after we receive the returned item.

## Lost Receipt
If a customer has lost their receipt, we can look up the order using the
order number, the email used at checkout, or the card used for payment.

## Warranty
All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects.
Accidental damage is not covered.
